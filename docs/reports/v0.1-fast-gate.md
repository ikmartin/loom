# The Fast gate, measured on a copy of relloc and on arXiv 2009.07720

2026-11-26, task T032 of `docs/plans/0.32-v0.1-remaining-task-list.md`. The gate is the Fast row of `docs/plans/0.32-v0.1.md`; its numbers are provisional. This report gives timings, sizes and counts only; nothing of either paper's contents.

**Scope, stated first.**

- **What was measured is `main` at 74d1a88b**, run from an archive of its `loom/src`, with the viewer it vendors. The two fixes this task made (below) were measured beside it and are not in the gate's numbers unless a row says so.
- **The build's first cut is not finished on `main`.** Built: its first session (b6acb022: a fragment's marks, an agent copy's comparison and a section draft's rendering made once while their inputs stand) and the records' two pure pieces from the listing session (0c974787: an annotation's body rendered once and a key's text hashed once for its text). Not built: `Records.apply` memoised per key, and the adoption fingerprint narrowed to the comparison's own inputs, which waits on the author's decision in the plan's queue item 60 (what a choice in Review is bound to). Taking TeX off the save, the one change that could bring the new words on screen within 5 s, is the second cut's (FAST-10 a and d, and the spike's changes 2 and 5), and what the reader sees meanwhile is also the author's decision in item 60.
- **The early answer on a write (T031) is on `main`** and is what the write rows measure.
- **The two quilts.** relloc: a copy of the author's quilt as he upgraded it to format 2 on 2026-11-26, copied whole, its `serve.json` and lock files removed; its `\externaldocument` lines are absolute paths into the original quilt, so a compile of the copy reads the original's aux files (read only; harmless for timing). The second paper: arXiv 2009.07720's TeX source, one file of 10,037 lines, with sixteen PDF files beside it in the quilt's folder, made into a quilt by the author with `loom init --from … --ai none -y --fix-anchoring` in a scratch home; its document has 251 blocks and 227 keys, and draws 197,000 elements in the viewer against relloc's 55,600.
- **The showcase's line** (a new word seen within 1 s) was measured on a copy of `demos/showcase`, in its atomic document's node `sh-0007`.

**The machine.** 8 cores, 16 GB, TeX Live 2024. Other sessions ran test lanes for most of the run: the one-minute load went from 2 to 58. Every number has the load beside it, and each line was measured again in a quiet stretch (load 2 to 8, 17:52 to 18:45) where it could be; the quiet numbers are the ones the verdicts use.

## Method

- **Saves.** `loom serve`, compiles on, on a warm copy, timed by its own event stream (`/_api/stream`). A word never written before goes at the start of the second line of a proof, or the first line of a statement, alternating, in the default document's own file (relloc's `drafting/draft5.tex`, 1,315 lines, 63 blocks; the paper's one file). At each `manifest` event the default document's page is fetched and the word looked for: *seen* is the first publication that holds it, *typeset* the first that holds it outside a `pre.source`. Each save then waits until the compile queue is idle.
- **Writes from the viewer.** A note through `/_api/annotate` as the viewer sends it: *answered* when the answer arrives, *published* when the stream's `built` event names its publication. Then a note sent 1 s after a save, while the save's build runs.
- **Agent calls.** As loom's MCP server runs each tool: `python -P -m loom …` in the quilt, `AI_AGENT` set, progress off, a server running beside it.
- **First open.** A copy with no `.loom/cache` (relloc) or as `loom init` left it (the paper); `loom serve` started, the default document's page polled until it is served.
- **The viewer.** Headless Chromium at 1400 by 900 on the served default document. Input to paint by the Event Timing API (an entry exists only for an event that took 16 ms or more, so 0 means under 16 ms); long tasks by their observer over 15 s with nothing done; hover over ten reference links, ten clicks on proof toggles; 33 keys typed by Playwright straight after words are marked with Annotate on, as fast as it types them (0.07 to 1.5 s for all 33), and the comment field read 1.5 s later.
- **Memory.** The server's `phys_footprint` (what Activity Monitor shows) at rest; `loom/scripts/memory.py --tex` for twenty saves (thirty in one run).
- **Disk.** `lifetimes.condemn`, read only, now and as of two hours on: what under `.loom/` the build as it stands does not reach. The machine's `biber` by `loom doctor`.

## What misses, and why

**A save, 7 to 11 s where the gate asks 5.** The typeset block arrives with the first publication on both quilts: the save's build makes the edited block by TeX, so the words and their typesetting are one time and that time is TeX's. In process on the paper at load 6.5 to 7, a save's build is 7.2 to 7.5 s, of which rendering 6.4 to 6.6 s: one fenced TeX4ht run of about 2.1 s, sixteen `extractbb` calls, one for each PDF in the folder, of about 2.0 s, and all 251 blocks rendered again because a node's key hashes its file's whole text (FAST-7). relloc renders 63 blocks again for the same reason. The showcase's 3.1 to 4.2 s is the same TeX run on a small quilt. Only taking TeX off the first publication reaches 5 s on either quilt, or 1 s on the showcase (the second cut, and the author's decision in item 60).

**A write during a save's build, 6.8 to 16.4 s.** The early answer answers once the act is recorded, but the act takes the server's `publication_lock` first, and a save's build holds that lock through TeX. `docs/design/writes.md` ("What the rebuild may rely on") already names the cure as not built: the rebuild takes `writes.lock` only to scan, never through TeX, and an act takes `writes.lock` alone. That is Safe's lock, so it is not small.

**Keys typed after marking.** The viewer listened for the keys that start a comment only once loom had answered the mark (about 0.3 to 0.4 s at rest), so keys typed in between reached the page's shortcuts. Fixed here.

**Agent calls on relloc.** Every MCP call starts a new Python and scans the whole quilt: on relloc a scan in a fresh process is about 0.8 to 1.1 s of the 1.1 s, loading loom about 0.2 s (0.7 to 1 s at load 25), and `status`, `ai orient` and `lint` also resolve every annotation's quote again (`records.store.resolved` and `selectors.find_quote`: under a profiler 3.9 of `status`'s 5.8 s). The cure is an MCP server that answers from a process that keeps its scan, or a scan kept on disk between calls: not small.

**First open.** The first build makes every block's HTML and then, before it publishes anything, every Review preview: on relloc rendering 59 s and recording 311 s of the 384 s, on the paper 58 s and 118 s of the 190 s. The plan's Speed row already says the cure, not built: publish the default document before digests and previews. With this branch's second fix the paper's first open falls to 125 s (load 4.6 to 6; rendering 46 s, recording 75 s), still 35 s over.

**Memory on relloc.** Warm, the server rests at 100 to 108 MB, the plan's last measurement (103 to 115 MB) unchanged; after a cold first build it holds 153 to 175 MB, which the gate's line, taken at rest, does not exempt. Not chased here.

**Disk.** A build writes Review previews, snippets and pictures that the next build no longer names, and only the sweep after the hour removes them (the queue's item 43). On relloc after a working session that is 40 % of the cache.

**A click on the paper under load.** A proof's toggle lays the 197,000-element document out again; 56 to 96 ms at load 3 to 6, over 100 ms at load 20. Not chased here.

## Larger causes, not fixed

1. TeX on a save's first publication, and every block of the edited file rendered again (FAST-7): the second cut, with the author's decision in item 60.
2. The first open publishing nothing until every Review preview is made: the Speed row's "the default document before digests and previews".
3. A viewer write waiting behind a save's build: an act takes `publication_lock`; `writes.md` names the cure.

6. What a build writes and no longer names staying until the sweep after the hour (item 43).
7. Not built from the first cut: `Records.apply` per key, and the narrowed adoption fingerprint (item 60).
