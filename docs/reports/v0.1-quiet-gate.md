# The Quiet gate, measured on copies of the author's quilts

2026-11-26, task T041 of `docs/plans/0.32-v0.1-remaining-task-list.md`. The gate (`docs/plans/0.32-v0.1.md`, Quiet): nine rows in ten that Review draws are a change the person would act on, each showing the change, and the person's own edits in no row. This report gives counts and kinds only; nothing of the quilts' contents.

**Scope, stated first.** Of the three settings the gate names, only the first ran: copies of the author's quilts. The mirrored quilt with a simulated co-author and the new quilt after a week of an agent's work are not this run. Both of the author's quilts were measured: the learner's quilt (quant), and the paper's quilt (relloc), whose scratch copy was in layout 1 and was upgraded by the author himself, since `loom upgrade` refuses an agent's shell; relloc was measured with main after T055. Problems, the gate's second clause, was not measured.

## Method

Each quilt was copied whole into a scratch folder, its `serve.json` and lock files removed, and read by this branch's loom with HOME and the XDG folders in scratch, the person's loom configuration copied there so that Review reads the look and acceptances under the reviewer name the author's own server uses. After `loom build --no-compile`, the rows were listed as the viewer draws them: `review-list`'s kinds as `evidence.review.gather` computes them (questions, fetch, cited, check, attest, attested-changed, and what waits for an agent), and the two kinds the viewer draws from the manifest (`viewer/src/lib/views/review/yours.ts`): Results to accept (blocks changed since accepted or taken in; the never accepted and the accepted are folded and count nothing) and Proposed edits (every change in `changes`, and each agent copy waiting). The server's own `review-counts` agreed with the manifest's count. Each row was judged by hand against the gate: a change the person would act on (keep or discard, accept, answer, check, attest, add a paper), or noise (a duplicate, loom's own bookkeeping, a stale or self-resolving item, a row about text that did not change, a change drawn so the person cannot read it).
