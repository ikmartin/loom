The gate (`docs/plans/0.32-v0.1.md`, Legible): the walks of `raw/walk.md` and `raw/walk-small.md`, by fresh agents who read only the introduction, at 1,440 and 760 pixels, on the showcase and on a new quilt made from an ordinary paper. Tasks 3, 4, 7, 12, 14 and 15 succeed for both walkers; the stronger achieves 17 of 18; every question of the author's list (H) has an answer on screen; no screen shows a terminal command. The colleague's walk is T039's, not this one.

**Verdict: fails.** One cause blocks most of it: with no name saved, every comment, annotation and Keep is refused with "Loom could not do that." (fix 1). A command shows on screen (fix 3). Task 12 fails on the new quilt, and task 8 fails everywhere.

### How it was run

- **Walkers:** fresh Haiku agents. Each played a postdoc who had read only the README's introduction (Install, Quilts, Your agent, Alpha). Each walk was split into two or three parts, run one after another on the same browser, because one Haiku context cannot hold eighteen tasks of screenshots. All walkers were Haiku, so the gate's "stronger walker" is not measured here.
- **Browser:** each walker drove its own headless Chromium through a small Playwright driver, deciding every action from the last screenshot. The driver gave it screenshots, the visible page text, and a list of controls; icon-only controls were listed without their hidden names. A tooltip showed only on a hover. Drops were synthetic DOM drops. The Mac's file dialog cannot be shown this way, so "or choose a file…" went untested and walkers dropped instead.
- **Viewer:** `loom app` and `loom serve` ran from this worktree's loom with HOME and every XDG folder in a scratch folder. The harness took the scratch HOME without complaint. Loom itself refused `loom app` while the shell carried `AI_AGENT`, so loom ran in an environment scrubbed of the agent markers, as `scripts/bench` runs it.
- **Quilts:** showcase walks started on "Your quilts" and opened a scratch copy of `demos/showcase`. New-quilt walks started on an empty home, made a quilt with New quilt, and dropped the source archive of arXiv 2012.12270 (one `.tex`, a `.bst`, a `.bbl`, five figures). A compiled arXiv PDF stood in for "a paper you read".
- **Pass and fail:** a walker's DONE counts as a pass. A walker's word was checked against the screen where it decided a row: the name refusal, the Keep and its undo, the command in the agent panel, and the library of the new quilt.
