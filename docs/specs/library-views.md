# What the library's views ask of the backend

**Partly built.** What §7's table and the Opens mark built is, on the node store's phase 2 and round six's fixture; the rest waits on the library's later phases. It specifies every read and every write the viewer's four views make once the library is rebuilt as [`docs/design/library.md`](../design/library.md) and [`nodes.md`](nodes.md) describe it: what each is given, the shape of what comes back, where the answer comes from, and what it may cost. The views are the sixth round of their mock-up, which the author settled on 2026-11-21, kept at [`docs/design/mockups/library-round-6/`](../design/mockups/library-round-6/README.md) with his marks and the rulings made after it; a screen is cited by its name in the prototype's strip (1, 1 thumbs, 2 choose, 4f, 7a search, 11b), and where a ruling changed a screen, this page follows the ruling. The backend builds to this page, and the viewer is built against fixtures shaped as it says until each read exists. About a node file `nodes.md` wins; about what the viewer is sent, this page. A paragraph that begins **Open** marks a place where the mock-up shows something the design or the node format cannot yet answer, with a proposal; §9 lists them by number.

## 1. Ground rules

Two principles of the design (`library.md`, "Scale") govern every read.

- **The viewer is sent what it is showing, and no more.** Nothing of the rebuilt library is in the manifest. A view asks for what is on its screen, a list of works, a work's outline, a result and what it uses, the rows of one Review list, and is sent that; what is folded or scrolled away is asked for when it opens.
- **Nothing is made before it is asked for.** A work's survey, its page text and word boxes, a thumbnail, a node's place on its page, a node drawn by TeX: each is made the first time a read needs it and kept by the hash of what it was made from, so it is made once.

Three of the views' rules reach the backend. No id and no command is drawn, so every answer carries the words a row is drawn with beside the ids it is addressed by. Nothing not yet checked reads as checked, so every status is computed from the evidence records on the node's current hash, and none is stored. No control sends the person to another view, so every read is answered for the view's own place: a result that points into another work opens that work in a Library tab, and a document is shown over Review, not in Docs.

**Sizes.** Costs are stated at the sizes in use: a library of 300 works; a work of 60 results; a Review of 30 to 40 rows; a web of some thousands of nodes, the drafts' results and the library's together. A cost is a budget measured with `scripts/bench` (never a stopwatch inside a test, `docs/contributing.md`), and, where one can be stated, a structural claim a test holds: what the read must not open or run.

**Sources.** Each answer says where it comes from:

| source | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| index | the node index loom derives from `.loom/library/nodes/` and the tag log: SQLite with full-text search, under `.loom/cache/` |
| evidence | records of acts on nodes, each naming a node's hash: digested (by whom, how), transcription verified, Lean observed, attested; corrections proposed; requests for a paper |
| scan | the scan of the person's documents the publisher already holds: their results, labels, numbers, citations, edges, fragments, the cite labels of their last compile (`tex/aux.py`, `read_cite_labels`) and the acceptance ledger |
| tasks | agent tasks: what was asked of an agent, and whether a session is working on it |
| store | the stored documents, by hash, and what is derived from them when first asked: a TeX source's survey (§3.2), page text, word boxes, thumbnails, nodes' places |
| annotations | the annotation log: notes, marks drawn on pages, agents' questions |
| changes | the change ledger and the compile state (`write-api.md`, `changes` and `compiles`) |
| lean | what loom observed when it ran Lean, fenced (`library.md`, "Formalization"); not built before the formalization phase |

## 2. Conventions

**Transport.** A read is `POST /_api/<name>` with a JSON body and a JSON answer, as `locate` and `compare` are: a handler in its family's module under `loom/src/loom/render/endpoints/`, listed in that module's `READS`, so the publisher does not rebuild after it, with the token and the checks every request to `/_api` takes (`write-api.md` §3), and a case in `cases_<family>.py`. The names follow `reserved.md`'s prefixes: `library-` for the library, `review-` for Review, `formal-` for formalization; the Web's reads take a prefix of their own, `web-`. Each is named in `GET /_api`'s `capabilities` once it is served, and a viewer that does not find one draws nothing that needs it (`write-api.md` §1). Rendered content is never in an answer (`write-api.md` §2): a draft's result is drawn from its fragment, whose path an answer gives, a change from fragments marked as `compare` marks them, and a library node from its LaTeX, which the viewer draws (§3.4).

**Bytes.** What never changes once made is a `GET` at a content address, as `/build/` is: no token, since pdf.js and an `<img>` fetch by URL and send none, served with `Cache-Control: immutable` and, as everything outside the viewer's bundle is, `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox` and `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` (`write-api.md` §3). A hash in an address is its 64 hexadecimal digits, without the `sha256:` an answer's field carries. `GET /_api/library/pdf/<hex>` is a stored PDF by its `pdf` hash; `GET /_api/library/thumb/<hex>.png` its first page (§3.1); `GET /_api/library/drawn/<node-hex>-<macros-hex>.svg` a node drawn by TeX, named by its own hash and its macros node's (`none` for a work with none), drawn only through an `<img>`, since it is TeX's output from a source nobody at loom wrote, and only for a work the person has compiled here (§3.4); for any other it is a 404.

**Freshness.** Every answer carries `gen`, the library's generation: a number the publisher raises whenever anything a read on this page answers from changes, a node file, the tag log, an evidence record, a task, a mark, an annotation on a work or a node of the library, a stored document, or what the scan gives these reads (results, labels, numbers, citations, edges), but not a note on a draft (the review's M-29), nor a publish that changes none of these, so a compile does not set every open view reading again. The stream (`GET /_api/stream`) sends an event `library` carrying `{"gen": N}` each time it is raised, and every library write answers with the `gen` it left (built by the node store, `write-api.md` §6). A view asks again for what it shows when it hears one; `library-works`, and `library-places` asked for a whole work's annotations, given `"since": N` while the generation is still N answer `{"gen": N, "unchanged": true}` and do no other work. Every other read answers in full whatever `since` says, since another ask under the same generation answers otherwise (the review's M-21). A node file an agent's command writes outside the publisher raises it too, so the publisher's watcher follows `.loom/library/` and `.loom/records/`. The obligations in `README.md` that make the manifest the viewer's only index and its change the only signal widen, once this is built, to these reads and this event.

**Names.** `ref` is a reference's id; `node` any node's id; `key` a draft result's key as the manifest names it, its id, or the qualified key of one with no label; `result` either a node or a key, since a library stem and a draft's id are minted from one namespace and never equal; `item` a result of a TeX source's survey (§3.2); `doc` a document's path; `row`, `mark`, `task`, `question`, `correction`, `citation`, `observation` and `undo` opaque ids a write is given back. Ids are for addressing and are never drawn. Whatever the viewer draws comes with its words:

- a work: `short` ("Smith 2024", "Halberd and Ravel 2021", "Vance et al. 2023"; for an entry that names no author, its title, which for a dropped file is the file's name), `title`, `authors` (surnames, in order), `year`;
- a result: `name`, its taxon's word and its number as its source prints it ("Lemma 3.2", "Definition 2.1"; the word alone when unnumbered), and `title`, its name in the source ("Test lemma"), absent when it has none;
- a document: its path, drawn as its file's name, as the file tree draws it.

A title and authors come from the reference's BibTeX body decoded: braces dropped, accents made characters (`Kova{\v{c}}` is "Kovač"), mathematics kept between `$` for the viewer to draw. Words that are loom's own, a hover card, a legend, an empty state, a confirmation, a task's sentence, are never sent: they are the viewer's, in `viewer/src/lib/words/`, a file a view.

**Status.** One vocabulary on every surface (`library.md`, "The views"), two marks a result. A read passes on the status `evidence.md` §5 computes, and the viewer draws it in round six's marks; nothing here computes a status of its own:

| field | values | from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `paper` | `evidence.md` §5.2's `reading`, `stub`, `unchecked` or `matches`, or `marked` for a place the person marked to digest and nothing read from yet; null for a draft's result and for a survey item not digested | evidence, annotations |
| `reading` | `evidence.md` §5.2's `reading` of a library result's paper, `stub`, `unchecked` or `matches`; null for a draft's result | evidence |
| `lean` | `{"lean", "role", "waiting_on"}` as `evidence.md` §5.2 gives them, `lean` one of `none`, `rerun`, `failed`, `unattested`, `changed`, `waiting`, `formalized` and `role` one of `proved`, `unproved`, `assumed`, `defined`, or null where no record names it (`{"lean": "none", "role": null, "waiting_on": []}`); in every row about a result, a list's and Review's alike (`evidence.md` §6, §8); null while formalization is off for the quilt | evidence |

The viewer draws `matches` as round six's filled circle ("matches the paper"), `unchecked` as the amber ring ("not yet checked against its paper"), `stub` as the dotted ring ("not read") and `marked` as the flag ("marked by you"); and `lean` as the filled diamond where it is `formalized` ("formalized") or the half-filled one where it is `formalized` with the role `assumed` ("assumed in Lean"), the amber diamond where it waits on the person: `unattested`, `changed`, or `waiting` with some `why` in `waiting_on` `unchecked` or `unattested` ("Lean accepted, not attested"); and the outlined one otherwise, `waiting` on reading alone among them ("not formalized"), and no mark for `none`. Where a result itself is shown, the Web's focus card, Review's opened row for it and the formalize panel, `waiting_on` is said in words ("not formalized yet: Lemma 2.1 of Okafor 2022 is not compared with its paper"); in a list it is the mark alone. The words are the viewer's; "matches the paper" was decided with the author for the person's yes to a transcription, and "not yet checked against its paper" before it, which the author renamed from "not compared yet" on 2026-11-25 so that it says what Review's Results to check asks. `formalized` holds only when nothing the result rests on in Lean waits on the person (`library.md`, "Digesting and checking"), which `evidence.md` computes as `waiting`.

**A result in a list.** Wherever results are listed (the Web, a neighbourhood, a node's uses, the formalize panel), a result is sent as `ROW`:

```json
{"result": "tq-00K3", "name": "Lemma 3.2", "title": "Test lemma", "taxon": "lemma",
 "source": {"ref": "tq-00A1-ref", "short": "Smith 2024"}, "paper": "unchecked", "reading": "unchecked",
 "lean": {"lean": "none", "role": null, "waiting_on": []}}
```

`source` is `{"doc": "drafting/main.tex"}` for a draft's result. A work's outline is the exception, since it also lists what is not yet a node (§3.2), and so is a Review row (§4.2). A work's results are in paper order: by the place the placement records give, once digesting fills them; until then by the node index's rule, number read naturally (3.2 before 3.10 before A.1) before pages, since a stub has a number and no page (ruled with the node store).

**What a refusal says.** A refused read or write is drawn in the viewer's words, a sentence for each code (`viewer/src/lib/words/frame.ts`, `refused`), and a plain one for a code it has none for. Loom's own message is never drawn: it is written for the command line, and may name a setting, a request's field or an id. A test reads every code the write API's table names for the library's endpoints, and every one loom raises there, and fails on one with no sentence.

**Who writes.** Every write this page adds is the person's and is refused to a declared agent with `author-only` (403), as the intake's are (`write-api.md` §6); the built writes it uses keep their own rules. An agent changes the web by its own commands (`library.md`, "What an agent can do to the web"). Errors are the write API's: a name that is not there answers 404 with `no-such-` and what it named (`no-such-ref`, `no-such-node`, `no-such-result`, `no-such-row`, `no-such-task`), a request refused on its merits 400, a conflict with the quilt's state 409.

**A quilt an earlier loom made.** Where `GET /_api` answers `format.found` below `format.reads` (`write-api.md` §1), the publisher waits on the quilt and serves only `library-upgrade`, and the frame draws only the column's head, one sentence, "This quilt was made by an earlier version of loom; upgrading it carries its papers over to this one.", and one button, `upgrade the quilt`, which is `library-upgrade`. While it runs, and if it is refused, a sentence of the viewer's says so ("Only the quilt's author can upgrade it.", for `author-only`), never the publisher's message, which names files and a command. An upgrade whose `carried.problems` is not empty says how many things could not be carried over, and the button then opens the quilt; otherwise the page loads again at once, and the publisher builds the quilt as it does any.

## 3. The Library

### 3.1 The works list (1, 1 thumbs, 1 add, 1 digest, 1c, 1d)

**Drawn.** In the column, the filters All works, Cited in your drafts and No PDF or source; the results not yet checked against their paper are Review's (§4, `check`). Above the list, a search by title or author, `list` and `thumbnails`, `add work…` and `digest…`. The list's columns are Title, Authors, Year and Digested ("11 of 62 · 2 checked"; "3 · 0 checked" for a work held only as a PDF), and from the columns menu Loom has and Used in; any column sorts. A click opens a work in a tab; ⌘-click selects without opening; on a row, `digest…` and a menu of `open its source ↗`, `edit its entry…` and `delete`. Thumbnails draw each work's first page, or "no document".

**Read `library-works`.** Given `{"doc"?: DOC}`; `doc` only from the Docs picker (§6.1). Answers every work:

```json
{"gen": 41, "works": [
  {"ref": "tq-00A1-ref", "short": "Smith 2024", "title": "Widgets and their bounds", "authors": ["Smith"], "year": "2024",
   "holds": {"pdf": "sha256:41b7…", "tex": true},
   "digested": {"statements": 11, "of": 62, "compared": 2, "stubs": 1},
   "used_in": ["drafting/bounds.tex"],
   "link": "https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01234v2",
   "version": null, "lookup": null, "cited_as": null, "cited_from": null}
]}
```

- `holds.pdf` is the stored PDF's hash, which addresses its thumbnail and its document, or null; `holds.tex` says loom holds the work's TeX source.
- `digested` is null when nothing of the work is digested. `statements` counts the work's statements that are not stubs, `compared` those whose `paper` is `matches`, `stubs` its stubs, and `of` the results its TeX source holds, from the survey (§3.2), null until a survey exists.
- `used_in` is the documents that cite the work, by an entry key their bibliography resolves to its reference.
- `link` is the work's own page for `open its source ↗`: the entry's `url`, else its DOI's resolver, else its arXiv page; null when it states none.
- `version` is Open 3; `lookup` is null or `{"state": "unconfirmed", "identifier": "arxiv:2209.01234v2"}`, the identifier `scheme:value` with the scheme lower-case, which the viewer draws as "arXiv 2209.01234v2" (1d, below); `cited_as` is null here and is the picker's (§6.1). `cited_from` is the work whose bibliography loom made this one from when it digested that work (`nodes.md` §6), drawn apart from the works the person brought; null for any other.

Filtering, sorting and searching are the viewer's, over the rows it holds: "Cited in your drafts" is a work some draft cites (`used_in`); "No PDF or source" is no PDF and no TeX source.

A delete a killed act left under way, its work standing without its notes, is finished by the next read of the library, and `library-works` answers it once, as `"deleted": {"undo", "ref", "short"}` beside the rows, so the viewer says it in the column with its undo, as it does after `library-delete` ("Deleted Smith 2024, with its results and notes", with `undo`), whichever view read the list first; every other read finishes it and says nothing (the review's M-25).

From: index (the references and a count of nodes by `source`), evidence (`compared`), scan (`used_in`), store (`holds`, `of`). Cost: one answer of 300 rows, about 60 KB; one grouped query on the index and one pass over the scan's citations, 50 ms warm. It opens no node file, no PDF and no source, and makes no survey.

**Read `GET /_api/library/thumb/<hex>.png`.** The PDF's first page, 360 pixels wide (twice the card's width), made by poppler the first time it is asked for and kept as `.loom/cache/library/thumbs/<hex>.png`. The viewer asks for a card only as it scrolls into view, sixty at first. Cost: the first ask about 0.2 s and 25 KB, then a file read; 300 works keep under 8 MB.

**Open 2.** `of` for a work never opened. "11 of 62" needs the work's survey, which is made when the work is first opened or digested, never for the list. Proposal: a work whose source has no survey yet shows its count without "of".

**Open 3.** "Arden 2025 · second version" (1). Each version is a reference of its own and that two are one paper is a `same` record (`nodes.md` §7). Proposal: `version` is the entry's own `version` field when it has one, else `2` for the later of two references a `same` record joins, which the viewer's words draw as "second version"; nothing is inferred from titles alike.

**Writes.**

- `add work…` opens a panel with `choose files…`, a field for an arXiv number or DOI, and "or drop files anywhere in loom" (1 add). A file chosen goes as a dropped one does (§3.5). A pasted identifier is `library-take` with `{"items": [{"text": "2209.01234"}]}`.
- `digest…` above the list offers `results my drafts use, in every paper (14)` and `everything in the papers I select`; a row's `digest…` is the work's (§3.2). The counts are `library-digest-scopes`; the acts are `library-digest`.
- `open its source ↗` opens `link` in the person's browser; nothing is written.
- `edit its entry…` is `library-entry` (§7), offered where the work's own read gives the entry to start from (`library-work`), so not on the node store's phase 2: it opens the work, and the entry's fold under its head as a field, with `save` and `cancel`, a refusal said beside it ("Saved the entry of Smith 2024."). **Open 4.** Round six draws the item and not the editing. Editing rewrites the reference's body and so its hash, which stales every record naming the reference (the inventory's question 20). Proposal: the entry opens as text in the work's tab, and a change rewrites the body; an attestation's closure takes in the statements and definitions a statement uses and leaves references out, since an entry's fields are not what a statement means, so editing an entry stales no attestation.
- `delete` is `library-delete`, answered with an `undo` the column's message offers ("Deleted Smith 2024, with its results and notes"), which `library-restore` takes. Ruled with the spec's merge: deleting a work is never refused. It is one act with one undo: the reference, its macros node, its nodes, and the person's notes and marks on the work go together and `library-restore` puts them back whole; what cites the work then reads as a result whose work is gone (`library.md`, "What this replaces"), and the evidence records stay, naming hashes no file has. How a node of another work that uses one of its nodes stays a valid file (`nodes.md` §11, check 7), and whether gathering makes the reference again from a draft's bibliography, is the references lane's to settle in `nodes.md`.

**The empty library (1c).** A box: "Drop papers here to start your library", "PDFs, TeX sources, or a .bib file", and the same panel as `add work…`. **Open 6**, closed: the intake takes a dropped `.bib`, an entry a row, and a TeX source, a `.tex` file or an archive, besides PDFs and pasted identifiers (`write-api.md` §6); nothing is matched by title or author.

**A work found while the network is off (1d).** Right after a drop, the row reads "arXiv 2209.01234v2 · look it up this once?" with `yes` and `not now` (round six's "on arXiv" left out, since the identifier before it names the service); otherwise, "details unconfirmed · look up". Beside the identifier the row also names the work as it came in, its title, which for a file the intake could not look up is the file's name ("arXiv 2401.01234v1 b3.pdf"), unless that title is the identifier itself; two works stating one identifier are told apart by it. The publisher sends `lookup.state` `unconfirmed` for a reference made from an identifier with no look-up, and null once a look-up is recorded; whether to ask "this once?" is the viewer's, which asks only right after a drop of its own whose row said `unconfirmed` with an `identifier` (an `added` row carries the first identifier its entry states as `scheme:value`, `arxiv:2209.01234v2`, or null, whether or not it is unconfirmed; the viewer draws it as "arXiv 2209.01234v2"). `yes` is `library-look-up` with `"online": true`, the person's allowance of the network for that one look-up, which passes a quilt whose network is off or unasked; `look up` is `library-look-up`, refused `offline` (409) on a quilt whose network is off, or `network-unasked` where it has not said, either of which makes the row ask "look it up this once?" again; `not now` writes nothing. `online` is the same boolean on `library-take`, `library-answer` and `library-look-up`, absent leaving it to the quilt's `[library] online` (`write-api.md` §2, §6, "The network, act by act", as the node store's lane wrote it). **Open 7.** What makes a reference unconfirmed is not a field of its file, which holds no status. Proposal: the intake's record of the act that made the reference says how its details were found (`found_by`, §3.5) and whether they were looked up; the reference reads `unconfirmed` until a look-up's record follows.

### 3.2 A work's tab (2, 2 digest, 2 choose, 3)

**Drawn.** In the column: the title; "Smith · 2024 · PDF and TeX · entry ▸", which shows the BibTeX entry as text; `digest…`; then Contents, the paper's sections with their pages and the results under the section they stand in, each with its marks. A click on a section or a result goes to its place on the page. For a work with its TeX source the outline lists every result of the source, digested or not; one not digested has no mark. For a work held only as a PDF, "Contents, from the PDF's outline", with the person's marks under the section their page falls in, then "Cited, not yet placed": stubs with no place, "not read · locate in PDF", which opens the reader at loom's best guess (`library-find`, §6.2), where the person marks the place (a `to-digest` mark naming the stub, §3.3). A work with no document: "Loom has this work's entry and no document. Drop its PDF anywhere to add it." and `open its source ↗`.

**Read `library-work`.** Given `{"ref": REF}`:

```json
{"gen": 41, "ref": "tq-00A1-ref", "short": "Smith 2024", "title": "Widgets and their bounds", "authors": ["Smith"], "year": "2024",
 "holds": {"pdf": "sha256:41b7…", "pages": 31, "tex": true},
 "entry": "@article{Smi24,\n  author = {Smith, Jane},\n  …\n}",
 "link": "https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01234v2",
 "outline": {"from": "tex", "state": "ready",
   "sections": [{"section": "s3", "number": "3", "title": "Bounds", "page": 12, "level": 1}],
   "items": [
     {"item": "def:radius", "node": "tq-00K1", "name": "Definition 3.1", "title": "Radius", "taxon": "definition", "section": "s3", "page": 12, "paper": "unchecked", "lean": null},
     {"item": "i41", "node": null, "name": "Remark 3.5", "taxon": "remark", "section": "s3", "page": 13, "paper": null, "lean": null},
     {"item": null, "node": "tq-00K9", "name": "Lemma 8.2", "taxon": "lemma", "section": null, "page": null, "paper": "stub", "lean": null}]},
 "marks": [{"mark": "m-7f3a", "page": 6, "words": "Lemma 2.4. Let ⪯ be a median order…"}],
 "unplaced": [{"node": "tq-00C2", "name": "Theorem 2.3", "paper": "stub"}]}
```

- `outline.from` is `tex` when loom holds the source, `pdf` when it holds only a PDF, and null with no document. Each section and item carries `confirmed`: false where its number is loom's own reading of the source's counters, which neither the stored PDF nor a compile has confirmed (a work with no stored PDF, whose survey says `why: "no-pdf"`), drawn as a reading and never as the number the paper prints. For `tex`, `sections` and `items` come from the survey: an `item` is a result of the source whether or not it is digested, named by its label, else by `i` and its place among the source's results (stable for one source); `node` is the node made from it, or null; a stub of the work that the survey does not hold comes last with `item` null ("not found in its TeX source"). For `pdf`, `sections` is empty: the viewer reads the PDF's own outline from the document it already has (pdf.js), and places the work's nodes that have a place, and its marks, under the section their page falls in; `items` then holds those nodes, with `item` null.
- `outline.state` is `ready`, or `reading` while the survey's compile runs (below).
- `marks` are the person's marks to digest on this work, annotations whose `purpose` is `to-digest` (§3.3), that nothing has been read from yet. `unplaced` is the work's stubs with no `pages` and no place.

From: index, evidence, store (the survey, the stored documents), annotations. Cost: about 15 KB for a work of 60 results; 10 ms once the survey exists. The first open of a work with a TeX source makes its survey.

**The survey.** What a TeX source holds, read once for each source and kept under `.loom/cache/library/survey/<hex>.json` by the source's hash: its sections (number, title, level), its results (taxon, number, title, label, section, the span of the text in the source), each result's page, and the source's definitions. It is what digesting extracts from (`library.md`: "With the TeX source loom knows every result exactly, and can show the ones not yet digested"), so digesting an item is writing its node, not reading the source again. It is made in two steps. Parsing the source, about a second for a paper of 60 results, gives sections, results, taxa, titles, labels, and numbers emulated from the source's counters (`library/counters.py`, which follows only the numbering a source writes literally: `\numberwithin`, a counter set to a number, `\the<counter>` redefined); the outline answers with these at once, `state: "reading"`, `page` null, and each `name` from an emulated number. A compile of a marked copy of the source runs as a job, one at a time as library runs do today, and can take minutes; what it prints is only a hint, since a source can read the person's files into it. With the work's stored PDF, the PDF governs: a result's heading is the line identified by its kind and its own words (its printed name and a number, its first words after it, never past the next result's or section's line), or, for a result with too few words, by its place between two so identified where the source and the PDF hold as many results' headings between them; a heading's by its title, a number alone on its line kept only where it continues the headings found at its level; never by a number, which is read from the line once it is found; a contents page holds no place; what nothing identifies keeps nothing and is named. Without a PDF, a number is kept where it is loom's own reading and the compile agrees, and no page. The plan, by the rules round six draws, as ruled:

1. Each target is to be proved, unless the person chose to assume it for now.
2. From a result to be proved, every result it uses is reached, by its statement and by its proof; from one assumed or a definition to be stated, only what its statement uses.
3. A result reached that is formalized, or one of the person's that Lean accepted and that waits for attestation or changed since it was attested, has its Lean already and is not followed. A definition or a convention not formalized, the person's or the library's, is in `state`. Another of the person's is in `prove`. Another of the library's is in `assume`.
4. A target, or a result in `state` or `prove`, with no label is in `stops`. It is the only thing that stops the request.
5. `before` is what the gate will ask (`evidence.md` §5.2, §7) of the target's Lean once one stands, else of the Lean the request makes: everything that does not hold now, each once, with what is wrong. `now`, the person's: a library result not read (`not-read`) or not compared (`not-checked`), or, where its work holds no document to read or compare it with, that document to add (`no-document`); a label two texts answer to near the target (`ambiguous`), or a label or library result nothing answers to (`missing`); a Lean statement to attest that Lean has accepted on its text now (`attest`, or `attested-changed` where it was attested before something it uses changed), offered only where `formal-attest` would take it, and otherwise what stands in its way in its place. `after` the agent's work: a Lean statement to attest that the agent will write, the target's own, a library result's to assume or to state, and one of the person's assumed for now, that has none yet; a Lean that comes round, through what it rests on, to a result already asked of (`cycle`). `waiting`, which nothing done now settles: a citation in what the targets' statements use that names no result (`cite`), until loom resolves citations to results; a library result that names no work, until it is given one (`no-source`). It is derived from the gate's own function, so it names nothing the gate does not ask and leaves out nothing it does: it is empty exactly when the targets read formalized, and what it offers `now` the act takes.

The sentence's counts are the lists' lengths, and the sentence is the viewer's. A statement one click away is `library-node` with `brief` for the library's, the fragment for the person's. From: scan (the person's results, their labels and uses), index (the library's uses), evidence and lean. Cost: a walk over the targets' closure, 50 ms at thousands, under 20 KB.

**Write `formal-ask`.** `{"targets": [KEY…], "assume": [KEY…]}`, answered `{"gen", "tasks": [TASK…]}`, one task a target, refused `needs-label` (409) naming each result without one (`library.md`: "loom says that the result needs a label first, and the author adds it to his source by hand"). Nothing in `before` refuses it: an unread or unchecked library result does not stop a request to formalize, and only keeps the result from reading formalized (`library.md`, "Digesting and checking").
