# v0.1: the journey scripts

A companion to `0.32-v0.1.md`: the scripts for learning and for proofreading that its Complete gate plays through. Each is a table of steps, each step what the person does and what must be on screen for it to pass, so that a run says which step failed and what it showed. The steps come from the study's material: learning from the author's month of use (`docs/reports/v0.1-study/raw/quant-findings.md`, the QF items), proofreading from the Author of `docs/design/README.md` and the journeys walked in `raw/redteam-coverage.md` §4 (the RTC items), and both from the exploring afternoon (`raw/explore.md`, the EXP items). The last column names the finding a step checks, or the gate or decision of the plan it checks. Exploring is not scripted here: the gate must pass for learning and proofreading, and `raw/explore.md` is the material for a third script if one is wanted. Overleaf and Formalization pass their own done-when, not a step here.

## How a run plays them

- **Who plays.** For the gate, the author plays the person, and the agent is a real model in the Code tab of the Claude app. A rehearsal before that, by an agent playing the person, drives the viewer in a browser at 1,440 pixels, starts at L1 or P1 with loom already serving its home page under a HOME of its own, uses Claude Code in a terminal in the quilt's folder as the agent (loom sees it the same way, and the chip says "in a terminal"), and says in its report what it did differently from the gate.
- **The rules of play.** After the install line nobody opens a terminal. No loom command is typed, pasted or read off a screen, and nothing under `.loom/` is opened by hand. What is said to the agent is plain words: the suggested wording may be changed, but it names no loom command, tool or path inside `.loom`. A step at which the agent asks the person to run something, or the viewer shows a command, fails.
- **Materials.** Learning: a chapter of a textbook as a PDF (a scan if one is at hand, since many textbooks are), and two papers on its subject as PDFs. Proofreading: a paper of the player's own as its LaTeX folder or source archive, with more than one `.tex` file, a bibliography, a figure and cross-references, and its PDF as compiled elsewhere to compare against.
- **Recording.** Each run is a dated section of `docs/reports/v0.1-complete-gate.md`: each step passed, failed (what was on screen, with a screenshot) or stuck (what the person would have needed), and how long the journey took. A journey passes when every step does. A failed step becomes a task in `0.32-v0.1-remaining-task-list.md`.

## Proofreading

The person is a researcher with a draft, proofreading it before submission.

| step | you do | passes when | checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | On "Your quilts", New quilt; name it for the paper; Create; Open it. Drop the paper's folder or source archive on the drop area, or use "or choose a file…"; if asked "Which file is the paper?", choose it. | It reads "Bringing your paper in and typesetting it", then the paper opens typeset in Docs. Nothing asks you to install TeX or a package. | RTC-17 |
| P2 | Open the compiled PDF from the file tree beside the paper, and read the first section and the one with the figure side by side. | Theorem, equation and section numbers, cross-references, citations, figures and equations match the PDF. (This is also the gate's check that a pilot's own paper reads correctly.) | RTC-37 |
| P3 | Connect Claude as in L4. | As L4, and the panel's "What it may change" lists your paper's files among those it may only read. | Safe, decision 1 |
| P4 | Say: "Proofread section 2 of my paper. Mark each problem as a comment on the exact words, and put each small fix as a suggested edit I can keep or discard." | Comments appear on the open page without a reload, each on the words it concerns and with its type (Revision, Suggestion or Question). The fixes wait in Review under Proposed edits, each drawing the old words beside the new. In your editor, the paper's files are unchanged. | Attachable |
| P5 | Open each comment; reply to one ("I disagree: …"); resolve another. | Your reply shows under the comment; the resolved one leaves what is open. | — |
| P6 | In Proposed edits keep two fixes and discard one; then undo the last keep. | Each kept fix is in your file exactly as drawn, and the page shows it within seconds. The undo puts back the file's exact text; the discarded fix left the file alone. | Safe |
| P7 | In your editor, fix one problem a comment names in your own way, and save. Then say: "I fixed the problem in *that place* myself; check it." | The page shows your new words within seconds. Comments on sentences you did not touch stay on their words. Your edit appears in no Review list. The agent sees what you wrote there without your quoting it, and resolves or replies to its comment. | Quiet, anchoring |

| P9 | Say: "Check that each citation in section 2 says what I use it for." | The agent reads the cited works the library holds and comments on the citing words. A work it needs and could not fetch waits in Review under Papers to add with a link to its page; dropping its PDF anywhere in the quilt adds it. | RTC-26 |
| P10 | In Review, open Problems. | Every row is about your own text and says in words what is wrong and where; none names a command. | Quiet; `raw/walk.md` task 15 |
| P11 | In Review, open History. | The versions of the paper are there, the edits you kept among them, and opening an earlier one shows it beside the current text. The agent's session report says what it did in this journey. | EXP-5 |
