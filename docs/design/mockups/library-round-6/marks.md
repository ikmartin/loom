# Round six of the library mock-up: the author's marks on rounds three to five, and the rulings on them

The author clicked through the prototype and marked it on 2026-11-21. His words are quoted; what to draw follows each. Where he invited critique, the ruling is the integrator's and he will see it drawn. Everything not named here stays as round five has it. The critic's three findings on round five are folded in at the end.

Two rules that now hold everywhere:

- **Loom writes nothing into his TeX by itself.** He decided this for labels ("it should show an error and say that the thing needs a label first, the person should add the label manually"), and the same rule covers a citation's pinpoint. An agent may still propose an edit, which he keeps or undoes like any other.
- **Review is filtered from the side column; there are no tabs for kinds.** "Tabs suck, stick with the left sidebar." Delete the tabs arrangement.

## Library

1. The drag cover (1b). "Unclear to me what 'A paper an agent is waiting for is filed with it' means, what is 'it' in this sentence, the thing dropped into the library? The text in 1b is not clear." The sentence meant: if the dropped file is one of the papers listed under papers to fetch, loom attaches it to that entry and makes no second one. Say it so: first line "Drop to add to your library." A second line only while papers are waiting for their file: "If it is one of the 3 papers waiting to be fetched, it is attached to that paper."
2. "How are 1b and 1c accessed through the library? Clicking the add work doesn't seem to do anything." 1b shows only while a file is dragged over the window, and 1c only in a library with no works, so neither can be reached by a click; say so in the notes strip. But `add work…` must do something: it opens a small panel under the button with what the empty library's box offers (choose files, paste an arXiv number or DOI, "or drop files anywhere in loom").
3. A work's side column. "The results list in the sidebar on a work in library is good, but it should be unified with a table of contents. Clicking one should jump to it in the text, sections should divide out the results." One outline: the paper's sections as headings, its results under the section they stand in; a click on either goes to its place on the page. For a work held only as a PDF, the sections are the PDF's own outline where it has one; otherwise the results alone.
4. The digest control. "The digest dropdown looks like a selector, i.e. a dropdown, because of the ∨ looking character. Instead it's a selector for digesting things in the document, and the text in the popup makes this unclear. Maybe something like digest... in the button, and then ...results used in my writing, ...things I marked in this document, ...results I chose, ...everything in <document name>." Ruling: his idea, tightened. The button reads `digest…` with no chevron. The menu's items finish the sentence, without leading dots, each with its count: `results my drafts use (4)`, `what I marked in this paper (2)`, `results I choose…` (which starts choosing in the outline), `everything in Smith 2024 (62)`. One quiet line at the foot says who does the work: for a paper with TeX source, loom, now; for a paper held only as a PDF, an agent, and it waits in Review. In the works list the same button offers `results my drafts use, in every paper (14)` and `everything in the papers I select`.
5. "I like the 'mark to digest | highlight | note | ask an agent...' selector in 3." Keep it.
6. "8a: Thumbnails view is great, include a thumbnails view in Library." The works list gets the same list and thumbnails choice as the picker in Docs, remembered.

Each name now says what waits and what is done to it: `Accepted results that changed`, `Agent questions`, `Attested results that changed`, `Cited results to digest`, `Digested results to check`, `Lean statements to attest`, `Papers to fetch`, `Proposed edits`.
9. "Hovering over any of the review queues should provide a text popup explanation for the queue and what its for." A hover card on each, as on the view names:
   - Everything: "All that waits for you, in one list."
   - Accepted results that changed: "Results you had accepted whose text has changed since. Read the change, and accept it again or not."
   - Agent questions: "Questions an agent asked while working. Answer with one of its choices or in your own words."
   - Attested results that changed: "Results whose statement, or a definition under it, changed after you attested the Lean. Say whether the Lean still states it."
   - Cited results to digest: "Results your drafts cite that loom could not read by itself. Have an agent read them, or locate one yourself."
   - Digested results to check: "Loom's copies of results, each waiting for you to check it against its paper."
   - Lean statements to attest: "Lean has accepted these. To attest is to give your word that the Lean statement says what your result says; only then does the result read 'formalized'."
   - Papers to fetch: "Papers an agent needs and could not download. Open each page, download the paper, and drop the file anywhere in loom."
   - Proposed edits: "Changes an agent proposes to your text. Keep or undo each."
   - Waiting for an agent: "Work you asked of an agent that none has finished. Nothing here needs you."
   - Drafts: "Show only what concerns one draft."
